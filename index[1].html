The Problem

Solar panels receive changing sunlight throughout the day, while many installations use a fixed orientation.

Traditional fixed panels

A fixed panel remains in one orientation after installation. As the Sun's apparent position changes, the angle of incoming sunlight changes too.

Why automation matters

  • Fixed orientation
  • Changing sunlight direction
  • Manual adjustment is impractical
  • Energy collection varies throughout the day
  • Advanced tracking can be complex or expensive

Our Solution

SunSync combines light sensing, control logic and motorized movement in one smart tracking concept.

How SunSync Works

A simple closed-loop tracking process.

01 • DETECT

Sense sunlight

Multiple light sensors detect relative intensity from different directions.

02 • COMPARE

Analyze readings

The controller compares sensor values to identify the direction with stronger light.

03 • MOVE

Adjust panel

The motor driver commands the motor mechanism to change the panel angle.

04 • RECHECK

Keep tracking

The system checks again and repeats the process as sunlight direction changes.

Single-Axis vs Dual-Axis

Different mechanisms can be considered depending on the application.

Single-axis tracking

  • Simpler mechanical design
  • Lower movement complexity
  • Good starting point for a prototype
  • Tracks primarily around one axis

Dual-axis tracking

  • Moves around two axes
  • Greater directional flexibility
  • More mechanical complexity
  • Potential future upgrade for SunSync

Safety & Reliability

A real-world product should be engineered and tested for safe operation. Possible protections include:

  • Motor movement limits
  • Emergency stop
  • Over-current protection
  • Mechanical limit switches
  • Weather-resistant enclosure
  • Automatic safe/return position
  • Protection during excessive wind conditions
